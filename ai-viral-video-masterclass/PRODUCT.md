## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (same as v1 and the Little Believers reference page). Fonts from Google Fonts; images inlined as data URIs.

## Users

Short-form video creators, freelance editors and small-business owners in South Africa (prices in rand, copy in English), usually arriving from a TikTok, Instagram or Facebook ad on a phone. Their job: decide in one visit whether this course will let them make the AI edits they keep seeing go viral, and buy on the spot.

## Product Purpose

An online video course that teaches two AI video effects start to finish: object swap (replace a car, outfit, background or sky while keeping light, reflections and camera movement) and motion transfer (apply the movement from one video to another person, character or photo). Six modules: setup and tools, shooting clips that work, object swap, motion transfer, editing for retention, posting and growing. Success for the sales page is an immediate purchase, ideally of Premium.

## Positioning

Two named effects taught from the raw clip on your phone to an export ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, with no editing background needed and no powerful computer (tools run in the browser or on the phone).
