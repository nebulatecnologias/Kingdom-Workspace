Course outline

Take a Look Inside the Masterclass

Six modules, in the order you'll use them.

  1. 01

    Setup and tools

    The AI video tools used in the course, what each one is best at, and how to set up your accounts on a budget.

  2. 02

    Shooting clips that work

    How to film source footage on a phone so the AI has clean edges, steady motion and good light to work with.

  3. 03

    Object swap, step by step

    Masking, prompting and matching light so the swapped object looks like it was really there.

  4. 04

    Motion transfer, step by step

    Picking a reference video, mapping the movement and fixing hands, faces and flicker.

  5. 05

    Editing for retention

    Before/after reveals, timing the cut to the beat, captions and sound so viewers watch to the end.

  6. 06

    Posting and growing

    Formats, hooks and posting routines for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus ways to sell these edits to clients.
