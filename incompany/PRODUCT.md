## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Vercel (same pattern as the other Kingdom sites in this workspace: `training/`, `tracktion/`, `academy/`). Multi-page site with clean URLs.

## Users

Business owners, directors and managers of companies and institutions in Mozambique (and South Africa), plus entrepreneurs starting a venture. They arrive when they need a concrete business problem solved by a partner: present the company, win a tender, structure strategy, sell more, digitise, or train a team. They evaluate whether Kingdom InCompany can be trusted with that work, then ask for a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Kingdom InCompany is the corporate arm of the Kingdom group: editorial development, strategic consultancy, marketing and sales advisory, technology development and corporate training. The website presents the four service categories, the method, proof (numbers, clients, media) and gets the visitor to request contact or book a conversation with a specialist.

## Positioning

"Um único lugar. Tudo o que você precisa para desenvolver o seu negócio." One partner that covers the document, the strategy, the sales engine, the technology and the team training, working through one method: Diagnóstico, Estratégia, Implementação, Autonomia. The engagement ends with the client autonomous, not dependent.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with specifics, never with inflated claims.
2. Every path ends in a conversation with a specialist.
3. The method (Diagnóstico → Estratégia → Implementação → Autonomia) is the spine of every service.
4. Built to grow: service pages and future sections plug into the same system.
