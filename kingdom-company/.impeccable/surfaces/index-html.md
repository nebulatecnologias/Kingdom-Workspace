# Página inicial · kingdom-company/index.html

Scope: home page of www.kingdomcompany.co.za. Mode: Persuade. Audience: gestores, CEOs e diretores (MZ e ZA), often on mobile from Instagram/LinkedIn. Job: understand who Kingdom Company is, find the service for their problem, ask for a quote (or book Business Advising). Sections pinned by the client, in this order: quem somos, o que fazemos, porque trabalhar connosco, metodologia, quem confia em nós, como nos contratar, perguntas frequentes, na mídia. Hero pinned by the client: slogan + «Conversar com um consultor» + «Conhecer os nossos serviços» (scrolls to services). Proof still to receive: client logos, testimonials, media links (placeholders only in preview; never invented). Group numbers not used.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The single partner a director can read in one pass: every question they ask, answered in their order, ending at a quote. Refuses the agency template of identical icon cards, stat heroes and kicker labels.

OWN-WORLD: Brandbook v2. Noite gradient hero with grain and diagonal light, the globe in light; marfim and white pages with hairline rules (#E8E4DC); índigo bands for method and closing; laranja only on primary actions; Google Sans 400/500/700; star pattern tone-on-tone in the method band.

STORY: Knows what Kingdom Company is → finds their problem among five areas with starting prices → trusts the method, proof and press → knows the four steps to hire → asks for a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width dark hero. Header with white logo, five anchors and a «Pedir cotação» button. Left: «Um único lugar.» at display size, the slogan's second line and the descriptor, two buttons (orange «Conversar com um consultor», outlined «Conhecer os nossos serviços»). Right: the globe, cropped, lit. Bottom edge: the five areas as quick links. Signature interaction: an area link scrolls to its row in «O que fazemos» and lights it.

FORM: Client-pinned classic hero plus ordered sections (overrides the roll); grounded list position 1 for the services index rows; seed key 4355ddfa (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
