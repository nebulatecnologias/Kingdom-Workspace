# Home Center — Dashboard de Gestão (surface brief)

Scope: the whole single-file prototype `clients/home-center/index.html`, all seven sections. Visitor mode: **Operate**.

Audience and task: Home Center leadership and store managers, during a sales demo, clicking through as if it were their daily console: read how each store tracks to target, act on today's alerts, answer messages per channel, follow orders, coach teams, measure campaigns and finance, and register the day's data in the same place they read it.

Constraints: Google Sans (pinned); lighter Home Center red (pinned); pt-MZ copy; illustrative data labelled as such; localStorage persistence only; Mídia Pro credit stays; must work at 390px without the nav eating the first screen, no clipped table columns, no truncated placeholders, no dead space under short cards.

Memorable moment: closing the day in "Fechar o dia" and watching the overview figures and store bars move to the new values, with an undo snackbar.

Unresolved: real partner name for the partnership campaign; billing-system integration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The store's whole day in one Google-Workspace-grade console where every figure names its source and target, and every section carries a "Registar" tab that writes into those same figures. It refuses the category default of a dark sidebar over a row of identical white KPI tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Material 3 light scheme seeded from a lighter Home Center red (primary around #E5383F, tonal containers in rose), warm neutral surfaces stepped by tone, not shadow. Navigation drawer with pill active indicator, rail at mid widths, bottom navigation on phones. Pill buttons (filled, tonal, outlined, text), filter chips with check marks, segmented buttons, outlined text fields with visible labels, snackbars. Google Sans everywhere, tabular numerals. Corners 8/12/16/28.

STORY: The manager understands in seconds which store is behind target and what is waiting today, acts on it from the alert itself, records the day, and sees the dashboard change in response.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top app bar with logo, store selector (segmented, applies everywhere), wide pill search, notifications, avatar. Left drawer. Section title with primary tabs (Painel | Fechar o dia). One tonal headline band of four figures, each with target and progress, followed by "Vendas por loja face à meta" beside "Precisa de atenção hoje"; the primary action "Fechar o dia" sits in the top-right of the section header.

FORM: Google Workspace / Material 3 interface language, position 2 on the ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK chosen by the user over the assigned WhatsApp Business grammar), seed key cde208bb, degraded roll without challengers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
