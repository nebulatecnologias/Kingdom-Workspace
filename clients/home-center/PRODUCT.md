# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step: `clients/home-center/index.html` plus `assets/` (styles, app logic, seed data, channel logos, Home Center logo), deployed to the Vercel project `home-center-dashboard-gestao` (team Nebula Tecnologias). Data entered in the prototype persists in the viewer's browser (localStorage) only.

## Users

Home Center's management in Maputo and Matola: the Direcção Geral and the managers of the three stores (Av. de Angola, Baía Mall, Matola), plus the corporate-accounts manager. They are the audience of a sales demonstration: the prototype is shown to them to win adoption of the system. During the demo, they click through as if it were their daily tool: checking how each store is doing against its target, answering customers, following orders and deliveries, coaching sales teams, and measuring campaigns.

## Product Purpose

A management dashboard for a furniture and home retailer with three stores and an online shop. It brings sales, store traffic, customer messages from every channel, orders and deliveries, team performance, customer satisfaction, campaign return and finance into one place. Success for the prototype: the Home Center leadership understands, by using it, that every number has a source, every alert leads to an action, and the daily routine (closing the day, answering messages, scheduling deliveries) can be done inside it.

## Positioning

Built around how Home Center actually sells: store visits counted at the entrance and converted by a scripted service routine (roteiro de atendimento, checked by mystery shoppers), WhatsApp per store as the main conversation channel, delivery plus assembly teams as the moment that decides satisfaction, and outdoor/partner campaigns measured by promo codes and QR. Payment methods are local (M-Pesa, e-Mola, card, transfer, cash, instalments); currency is the metical (MT).

## Operating Context

- Stores: Av. de Angola 2356, Maputo (main store, 45 sellers); Baía Mall, loja G69, Maputo (12 sellers); Rua 11.117, n.º 75, Matola (12 sellers); plus the website.
- Channels: WhatsApp of each store, Instagram, Facebook, Google reviews, website forms (including a corporate form).
- Service hours end at 17h30; out-of-hours messages get an auto-reply and enter the 8h00 queue.
- Rules in use: first response under 5 minutes; dissatisfied customers (NPS detractors) contacted within 24 hours; public reviews answered within 48 hours; discounts up to 4% of revenue; roteiro compliance target 85%.
- Delivery and assembly teams A–D; order pipeline: received → paid → in preparation → out for delivery → assembled.
- A 10-minute briefing before opening and weekly coaching for sellers below 70% on the roteiro.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Visão geral, Mensagens, Pedidos e entregas, Lojas e equipas, Satisfação e NPS, Campanhas, Financeiro.
- Data entry required in every area (confirmed): daily store close (visits, sales, value, customer origin); orders and deliveries (new order, status changes, delivery/assembly scheduling); campaigns and finance (investment and results, receivables, discounts); teams and satisfaction (mystery-shopper evaluations, NPS answers, detractor follow-up).
- Entered data must update the figures shown, and persist in the browser. No shared backend, no login.
- The message inbox filters by channel.
- Interface language: Portuguese as written in Mozambique (pt-MZ, European spelling: "acção", "facturação", "equipa").

## Brand Commitments

- Home Center logo (red "H" house mark with "HOME CENTER" wordmark), embedded in the page.
- Primary brand colour is red; the user asked for a lighter red than the incumbent `#D41F26`.
- Typeface: Google Sans (binding, requested by the user).
- Credit line "Protótipo desenvolvido por Mídia Pro – Comunicação e Imagem" stays visible.

## Evidence on Hand

All figures, customers, sellers, campaigns and invoices in the prototype are illustrative ("Dados ilustrativos para demonstração") and must stay labelled as such. The partner name in the partnership campaign is still to be confirmed ("[nome do parceiro]"). Billing-system integration is undecided. No real testimonials, client logos or metrics exist; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Every number states its source and its target, so a manager can trust it without asking.
2. Every alert ends in an action that can be completed on the spot.
3. The daily routine is entered in the same place it is read: closing the day changes the dashboard.
4. Compare the three stores side by side; the store filter applies everywhere.
5. Speak the store floor's language, not analytics jargon.
